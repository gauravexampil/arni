Graphic Design Course Details

The Graphic Design Course at Arni Computer Institute is designed to transform creative minds into skilled visual storytellers. Whether you're passionate about art or looking to enter the creative industry, this course will teach you how to design eye-catching graphics, logos, posters, social media creatives, and more using industry-standard tools.

  • Course Features:
  • 100% practical-oriented training
  • Taught by experienced industry professionals
  • Real-world projects and assignments
  • Certificate of Completion

Who Should Join:

  • Students and freshers passionate about design
  • Social media marketers and content creators
  • Business analysts and MIS executives
  • Entrepreneurs who want to create their own branding
  • Anyone looking to start a career in graphic design

What You’ll Learn:

Introduction to Graphic Design

Understanding design principles: color theory, typography, balance, contrast, and layout Exploring the role of graphic design in branding and marketing Design trends and inspiration.

Mastering Design Software

Adobe Photoshop: Image editing, photo manipulation, and digital artwork Adobe Illustrator: Creating vector graphics, logos, icons, and illustrations Adobe InDesign/CorelDRAW (optional): Layout design for magazines, brochures, and flyers..

Creative Projects

Business cards, brochures, flyers Logo and brand identity creation Web graphics and mockups

Portfolio Development

Working on real-time assignments Creating a professional portfolio to showcase your work Tips on presenting your designs for freelance or job opportunities

Interview Skills

Soft Skills, CV Prep, Body Language, SWOT Analysis, Grooming.
